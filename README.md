# The Vampire Diaries
- The Vampire Diaries is a supernatural drama series following Elena Gilbert, a high school teenager who falls in love with 162-year-old vampire Stefan Salvatore. Her life dramatically complicates when Stefan's vengeful older brother, Damon, arrives in their town of Mystic Falls, sparking a dangerous love triangle amidst an underworld of witches, werewolves, and ancient immortal threats.

| Main Characters | Description |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| Elena Gilbert | A high school student grieving the loss of her parents who finds herself torn between two vampire brothers while discovering she is a central piece in ancient supernatural prophecies. |
| Stefan Salvatore | A brooding, compassionate vampire who resists his dark bloodlust to protect human life and falls deeply in love with Elena. |
| Damon Salvatore | Stefan’s charismatic, impulsive older brother who initially arrives as a ruthless villain but evolves into a fiercely protective anti-hero. |
| Bonnie Bennett | Elena’s loyal best friend who discovers she comes from a powerful lineage of witches, frequently sacrificing her own safety to protect her loved ones. |
| Caroline Forbes | A vibrant, self-conscious high schooler whose transformation into a vampire brings out her inner strength, resilience, and tactical mind. |
| Matt Donovan | Elena’s dependable ex-boyfriend who remains steadfastly human, serving as the group's grounded anchor amidst constant supernatural chaos. |
| Tyler Lockwood | The local mayor's son who triggers a family curse to become a werewolf, eventually transforming into the first successful vampire-werewolf hybrid. |
| Alaric Saltzman | A high school history teacher and vampire hunter who becomes a surrogate parent and mentor to Elena and her friends. |
